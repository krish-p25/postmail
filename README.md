## Why PostMail

You send an important email and then... nothing. Did they read it? Is it worth following up, or are you about to look impatient? PostMail replaces that guesswork with a notification the instant it happens.

- **Real-time open tracking** — know the second an email is read, not just that it eventually was.
- **Works where you already work** — a lightweight Chrome extension adds tracking to Gmail and Outlook directly in the compose window. No new tab, no separate app to check.
- **Instant notifications** — get pinged on Discord the moment someone opens your email, so a follow-up can happen while it's still relevant.
- **A dashboard that just shows you what matters** — every sent email and every open, with noise like your own preview-loads and proxy fetches automatically filtered out.

## How it works

1. Compose an email in Gmail or Outlook as you normally would.
2. The PostMail extension quietly attaches a tracking pixel — invisible to the recipient.
3. Send it.
4. The moment it's opened, PostMail knows — and tells you, via the dashboard and your Discord notification.
